# Geekist Stars — research service

Deliver a useful research MCP from existing Stars capabilities, then develop richer evidence acquisition and reuse where actual research exposes a gap. Stars owns GitHub catalogue/discovery and associated research evidence. SaaS Research owns its investigation and accepted conclusions; Lumos owns its graph/CRM domain. Do not replicate those systems inside Stars.

## Delivery and learning paths

- S01 audits contracts and deployment; S02 establishes shared catalogue queries; S03 exposes and deploys a read-first MCP. These are the initial delivery path.
- S04 explores repositories beyond the catalogue; S05 adds targeted issue/discussion/release evidence. Both depend on the audit, not MCP deployment or one another. Coordinate shared contracts via agreed boundaries; do not assume their implementation must be monolithic.
- S06 runs a bounded research reuse specimen with existing capabilities, independently of MCP deployment. It exchanges evidence with SaaS Research M07 and feeds specific gaps into open tasks. It does not accept M07 or make it a cross-project gate.
- S00 is the operational reconciliation acceptance task for PR #6. It does not block research service design.

Initial tools should preserve curated membership, collection mentions, independent research results, and live GitHub observations as separate concepts. No automatic starring or list mutation from research; preserve existing explicit write authority. Prefer adapting the public SDK and existing transports over introducing another storage engine or a second implementation of the same query.
